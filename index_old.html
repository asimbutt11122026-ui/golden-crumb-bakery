Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

⏱️ 30 minutes | 🍪 Makes 24

Soft and chewy with crispy edges. A timeless favorite that's perfect with coffee.

View Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 225g butter, softened
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 100g white sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 280g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 340g chocolate chips

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cream butter and both sugars until fluffy.
  3. Beat in eggs and vanilla.
  4. Mix in flour, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Fold in chocolate chips.
  6. Drop spoonfuls onto baking sheet.
  7. Bake 9-12 minutes until golden.
  8. Cool on wire rack.

Artisan Sourdough Bread

⏱️ 24 hours | 🍞 Makes 2 loaves

Tangy and complex flavor with a beautiful crispy crust. Requires a sourdough starter.

View Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 500g bread flour
  • 350ml water
  • 100g active sourdough starter
  • 10g salt

Instructions:

  1. Mix flour, water, and starter. Let rest 30 min.
  2. Add salt and knead until smooth.
  3. Bulk ferment 4-6 hours with stretches every 30 min.
  4. Shape into round loaves and place in banneton baskets.
  5. Cold retard in fridge 12-18 hours.
  6. Score and bake in Dutch oven at 450°F for 45 min.
  7. Cool completely before slicing.

Classic Butter Croissants

⏱️ 20+ hours | 🥐 Makes 16

Crispy, flaky, and buttery. A labor of love that's absolutely worth the effort.

View Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 500g strong bread flour
  • 250ml whole milk
  • 7g instant yeast
  • 10g salt
  • 40g sugar
  • 250g cold butter (for lamination)

Instructions:

  1. Make dough with flour, milk, yeast, salt, and sugar.
  2. Bulk ferment 1 hour.
  3. Laminate with butter using 4 folds over 2 days.
  4. Shape into triangles and proof 2-3 hours.
  5. Egg wash and bake at 400°F for 20-25 min.
  6. Cool on wire rack to achieve perfect crispness.

Decadent Chocolate Cake

⏱️ 1.5 hours | 🎂 Serves 12

Rich, moist, and absolutely divine. Perfect for birthdays and celebrations.

View Recipe

Cake Ingredients:

  • 180g all-purpose flour
  • 60g cocoa powder
  • 300g sugar
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 200ml strong coffee
  • 120ml buttermilk
  • 120ml vegetable oil

Frosting Ingredients:

  • 200g butter, softened
  • 400g powdered sugar
  • 120g cocoa powder
  • 120ml heavy cream

Instructions:

  1. Mix dry ingredients and combine with wet ingredients.
  2. Divide into 2 greased pans and bake at 350°F for 30-35 min.
  3. Cool completely.
  4. For frosting, beat butter and sugar, add cocoa and cream.
  5. Layer cakes with frosting between and on top.

Glazed Doughnuts

⏱️ 3 hours | 🍩 Makes 12

Light, fluffy, and coated in sweet glaze. A morning favorite for a reason.

View Recipe

Dough Ingredients:

  • 500g all-purpose flour
  • 7g instant yeast
  • 60g sugar
  • 10g salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 120ml whole milk
  • 60g butter, softened

Glaze Ingredients:

  • 200g powdered sugar
  • 30ml milk
  • 30g butter, melted
  • 1 tsp vanilla

Instructions:

  1. Mix dough ingredients and knead until smooth.
  2. First rise 1.5 hours.
  3. Roll out, cut doughnuts, and proof 1 hour.
  4. Fry in oil at 375°F until golden on each side.
  5. Mix glaze ingredients and dip warm doughnuts.

Vanilla Cupcakes with Buttercream

⏱️ 45 minutes | 🧁 Makes 24

Soft, moist, and elegant. Top with any buttercream flavor you love.

View Recipe

Cupcake Ingredients:

  • 225g butter, softened
  • 225g sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 225g all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 120ml whole milk
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract

Buttercream Ingredients:

  • 225g butter, softened
  • 450g powdered sugar
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 2 tbsp heavy cream

Instructions:

  1. Cream butter and sugar until fluffy.
  2. Beat in eggs one at a time.
  3. Alternate adding flour mixture and milk.
  4. Fill cupcake liners 2/3 full.
  5. Bake at 350°F for 18-22 minutes.
  6. Cool, then frost with buttercream.
