Bake at Home: Our Favorite Recipes

Try our beloved recipes in your own kitchen. Each one is crafted with the same care and quality ingredients we use at Golden Crumb.

🍪

Classic Chocolate Chip Cookies

⏱️ 30 minutes | 🍪 Makes 24

Soft and chewy with crispy edges. Perfect with coffee.

View Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 225g butter, softened
  • 200g brown sugar
  • 100g white sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 280g all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 340g chocolate chips

Instructions:

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
  2. Cream butter and sugars until fluffy.
  3. Beat in eggs and vanilla.
  4. Mix in flour, baking soda, and salt.
  5. Fold in chocolate chips.
  6. Drop spoonfuls onto baking sheet.
  7. Bake 9-12 minutes until golden.
  8. Cool on wire rack.

Artisan Sourdough Bread

⏱️ 24 hours | 🍞 Makes 2 loaves

Tangy and complex flavor with a beautiful crispy crust.

View Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 500g bread flour
  • 350ml water
  • 100g active sourdough starter
  • 10g salt

Instructions:

  1. Mix flour, water, and starter. Let rest 30 min.
  2. Add salt and knead until smooth.
  3. Bulk ferment 4-6 hours with stretches every 30 min.
  4. Shape into round loaves and proof overnight in fridge.
  5. Score and bake in Dutch oven at 450°F for 45 min.
  6. Cool completely before slicing.

Classic Butter Croissants

⏱️ 20+ hours | 🥐 Makes 16

Crispy, flaky, and buttery. Absolutely worth the effort.

View Recipe

Ingredients:

  • 500g strong bread flour
  • 250ml whole milk
  • 7g instant yeast
  • 10g salt
  • 40g sugar
  • 250g cold butter (for lamination)

Instructions:

  1. Make dough and bulk ferment 1 hour.
  2. Laminate with butter using 4 folds over 2 days.
  3. Shape into triangles and proof 2-3 hours.
  4. Egg wash and bake at 400°F for 20-25 min.
  5. Cool on wire rack.

Decadent Chocolate Cake

⏱️ 1.5 hours | 🎂 Serves 12

Rich, moist, and absolutely divine.

View Recipe

Cake Ingredients:

  • 180g all-purpose flour
  • 60g cocoa powder
  • 300g sugar
  • 2 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 200ml strong coffee
  • 120ml buttermilk
  • 120ml vegetable oil

Instructions:

  1. Mix dry ingredients and combine with wet ingredients.
  2. Divide into 2 greased pans and bake at 350°F for 30-35 min.
  3. Cool completely and frost with chocolate buttercream.
🍩

Glazed Doughnuts

⏱️ 3 hours | 🍩 Makes 12

Light, fluffy, and coated in sweet glaze.

View Recipe

Dough Ingredients:

  • 500g all-purpose flour
  • 7g instant yeast
  • 60g sugar
  • 10g salt
  • 2 eggs
  • 120ml whole milk
  • 60g butter, softened

Instructions:

  1. Mix dough and knead until smooth.
  2. First rise 1.5 hours.
  3. Roll out, cut doughnuts, and proof 1 hour.
  4. Fry in oil at 375°F until golden.
  5. Dip in glaze while warm.

Vanilla Cupcakes with Buttercream

⏱️ 45 minutes | 🧁 Makes 24

Soft, moist, and elegant. Perfect for any occasion.

View Recipe

Cupcake Ingredients:

  • 225g butter, softened
  • 225g sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 225g all-purpose flour
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 120ml whole milk
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract

Instructions:

  1. Cream butter and sugar until fluffy.
  2. Beat in eggs one at a time.
  3. Alternate adding flour mixture and milk.
  4. Fill cupcake liners 2/3 full.
  5. Bake at 350°F for 18-22 minutes.
  6. Cool and frost with buttercream.
